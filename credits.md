The calculator, slide rule and punch cards on the desk are my drawings, made from
measurements of photographs of real ones: an HP-45 photographed by Joe Haupt, Sally
Ride's slide rule in the Smithsonian, and an IBM 5081 card scanned by Rjluna2.

The coffee ring is cut from [a photograph of a real one](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Coffee_Stain_P1000065.JPG)
by Nevit Dilmen ([CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/)), so that
image stays under CC BY-SA 3.0.
